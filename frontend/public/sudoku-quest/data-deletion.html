Data Deletion Request

Use the steps below to request deletion of data associated with Sudoku Quest.

1. App and Developer

This page applies only to the Sudoku Quest mobile game. Requests are reviewed by the support team. Even if the game does not require an account, you can still request deletion of any email- or device-linked records.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send an email to iammustafakucukkoc@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line “Sudoku Quest Data Deletion Request”.
  3. Include the email address you used in the app, your device/platform, and the data types you want deleted.
  4. We may ask for additional information if needed to verify the request.

Verified requests are usually processed within 30 days.

3. Data Types That Can Be Deleted

  • Cloud-stored game progress, level state, notes, daily puzzle history, and app preferences, if any
  • Email address and message content related to support requests
  • Technical records and notification tokens that can be associated with your account or device
  • In-app profile data or user identifiers, if any

4. Data That May Be Retained and Retention Periods

  • Some records may be retained as required for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, payment disputes, or accounting requirements.
  • Google Play or the relevant payment provider may retain payment, subscription, or purchase records separately.
  • Aggregated or anonymized statistics that cannot identify a user may be retained after a deletion request.
  • Residual backup copies are removed through routine cycles within 90 days.

5. Local Data on Your Device

Sudoku Quest may store some game progress or preferences locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data from device settings may remove this local data.

6. Contact

For questions about data deletion requests, contact iammustafakucukkoc@gmail.com.
